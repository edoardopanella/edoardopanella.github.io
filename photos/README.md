Put your pictures in this folder (JPG, ideally ~1600px wide and under 500 KB each), then list them in ../photos.json, e.g.

[
  {"src": "photos/milano-navigli.jpg", "caption": "Sunday on the Navigli", "place": "Milano", "date": "2025"},
  {"src": "photos/madison-lake.jpg", "caption": "Lake Mendota in winter", "place": "Madison", "date": "Dec 2025"}
]

The Photos window shows them as polaroids and opens a slideshow on click.
