    A network that learns without gradients.

    100 binary neurons with random, asymmetric connections learn to recognise six shapes. There is no backpropagation: every connection changes using only what its two neurons can see. A simplified, in-browser version of the rule in Badalotti, Baldassi, Mézard, Scardecchia & Zecchina (2025).

    Input · click to draw
    λxλy label
    Network · idle
    readout
    Output
    1. 1 · NudgeInput and the correct label push the neurons, which settle into a stable pattern.
    2. 2 · Let goThe label is removed. The network keeps running and settles again.
    3. 3 · StabiliseEach unstable neuron strengthens links that agree with it: Jij += η si sj. Purely local.

    Turn a pile of papers into a map.

    Mantis, built at MIT CSAIL and the Kellis Lab, lets you explore large collections of documents as a map. This is its core pipeline on 16 paper titles, running live in your browser: embed each title as a vector, project the vectors to 2D with UMAP, then find clusters. Add your own title and watch where it lands.

      Embeddings: WordLlama token embeddings (distilled from Llama 2), averaged and truncated to 64 dimensions, about 4,800 words bundled with the page. UMAP and k-means are small from-scratch implementations. The real Mantis runs far bigger models on millions of documents.
